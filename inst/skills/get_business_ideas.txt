## EXAMPLE INPUT

2024 is the global shift in supply chain strategies due to rising geopolitical tensions and the aftermath of the COVID-19 pandemic.
In 2024, many businesses have been moving away from heavily centralized, just-in-time supply chains and are adopting more resilient, diversified models. The disruptions caused by events like the pandemic, the war in Ukraine, and trade tensions, particularly between the U.S. and China, have exposed vulnerabilities in global supply chains.
This shift has led to the rise of onshoring and nearshoring, with companies bringing manufacturing closer to home or to regions with more stable political environments. Additionally, some businesses are focusing on building redundancy into their supply chains—having multiple suppliers for key components, for example, to avoid the risk of a single disruption shutting down production.
There’s also been a significant push toward supply chain transparency and technology adoption. Companies are investing in tools like blockchain and AI-driven logistics platforms to track products more effectively and respond quickly to disruptions.
This evolving landscape is reshaping industries like electronics, automotive, and consumer goods, as companies are rethinking where and how they source materials, manufacture products, and distribute them globally.
So, the major market change here is the way businesses are reworking their supply chains for greater resilience in an increasingly unpredictable global environment.

## EXAMPLE OUTPUT

1. Supply Chain Risk Management Software (AI-driven)

- Business Idea: Develop a SaaS platform that leverages AI and machine learning to predict potential disruptions in the supply chain. It would analyze a wide range of variables—geopolitical events, climate change, local labor strikes, raw material shortages, and more—and help businesses forecast and mitigate risks.
- Target Audience: Enterprises with complex global supply chains, especially in manufacturing, retail, and technology sectors.
- Features:
  - Real-time risk analysis based on current events (news, economic reports, etc.).
  - Predictive insights on disruption probabilities and suggested actions.
  - Integration with existing ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) systems.
  - AI-driven alerts for potential supply chain risks and alternative supplier recommendations.
- Design Considerations:
  - Intuitive dashboards that display risk levels by region, supplier, and product category.
  - A user-friendly interface with drag-and-drop scenario planning tools.
  - Mobile app for supply chain managers to monitor risks on-the-go.

2. Nearshoring Logistics Network

- Business Idea: Create a logistics and fulfillment service focused on supporting nearshoring efforts by businesses looking to shift production closer to home or to more stable regions. The service would provide a network of local suppliers, warehousing, and optimized shipping routes to reduce dependency on distant and vulnerable suppliers.
- Target Audience: Mid-to-large-sized manufacturers, especially in electronics, automotive, and consumer goods industries.
- Features:
  - A platform that connects manufacturers with vetted, reliable suppliers and logistics partners within closer geographical proximity.
  - Smart contract features to streamline supplier agreements and automate payments.
  -Real-time tracking and route optimization for shipping goods within regional networks.
- Design Considerations:
  - Easy-to-use platform for sourcing, contracting, and managing suppliers and logistics.
  - A map-based interface for route optimization and real-time tracking.
  - Customizable reporting tools for businesses to track costs, delivery times, and carbon footprint.

3. Blockchain-based Supply Chain Transparency Platform

- Business Idea: Launch a blockchain-powered platform that helps companies track and verify the origin of materials, production processes, and transportation of goods in their supply chains. This service would appeal to businesses wanting to increase transparency and sustainability in their supply chains to build trust with consumers and meet regulatory requirements.
- Target Audience: Consumer goods brands, food & beverage companies, and eco-conscious brands.
- Features:
  - Blockchain ledger to record every transaction and movement in the supply chain, ensuring transparency.
  - Supplier ratings and certification systems, allowing businesses to vet their partners.
  - Integration with IoT devices for real-time tracking of goods and environmental data (e.g., carbon emissions).
- Design Considerations:
  - A user-friendly interface for both consumers (to trace products) and businesses (to manage supply chain).
  - High emphasis on security and data integrity to build trust.
  - Mobile and desktop access for businesses and consumers to trace products from origin to shelf.

4. Sustainable Packaging Solutions for Nearshore Manufacturers

- Business Idea: Establish a company that offers eco-friendly, cost-effective packaging solutions for manufacturers transitioning to nearshoring. The company would specialize in designing packaging that meets the needs of regional production while minimizing environmental impact and aligning with sustainability goals.
- Target Audience: Small-to-medium manufacturers and brands that have switched to nearshoring and need localized, sustainable packaging solutions.
- Features:
  - Custom-designed, eco-friendly packaging materials such as biodegradable plastics, recycled paper, or reusable packaging options.
  - Supply chain integration for efficient packaging sourcing and management.
  - Consultation services for sustainable packaging design and compliance with local environmental regulations.
- Design Considerations:
  - Attractive, eco-conscious packaging designs that communicate sustainability to consumers.
  - Cost-effective, lightweight materials that reduce transportation costs.
  - A website with an easy-to-use packaging customization tool for manufacturers to design and order packaging.

5. Local Manufacturing & 3D Printing Service Hub

- Business Idea: Build a network of local 3D printing hubs in various regions to support nearshoring efforts. This service would allow companies to quickly produce small batches of components and products locally, using advanced 3D printing technology to reduce dependence on traditional manufacturing and long supply chains.
- Target Audience: Small and medium-sized businesses in industries like automotive, healthcare, and consumer electronics that need quick-turnaround production and prototyping.
- Features:
  - A platform where businesses can upload designs and specifications for 3D printed components.
  - A network of local printing hubs that can quickly produce parts at scale.
  - Options for using a variety of sustainable and durable materials in the 3D printing process (e.g., recycled plastics, metal alloys).
- Design Considerations:
  - A seamless digital platform for design submission, real-time collaboration, and order tracking.
  - Integration with CAD (Computer-Aided Design) tools for easy design uploads.
  - Geolocation-based recommendations for the nearest 3D printing hubs with the right materials and production capabilities.
